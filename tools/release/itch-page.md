## Description (paste as is)

You were a mapmaker in a remote village. Then the crown drafted you for a war, the raid party took a shortcut that was not on any of your maps, and the ground gave way.

**NO HOPE** is a short 8-bit dungeon roguelike for Windows. One run takes about 25 to 30 minutes. You fall into the dark, fight through the floors looking for a way out, and the dungeon starts over every time you die.

- **Timing-bar combat.** A bar sweeps across the screen: hit it in the bright zone for a Perfect. Skills are rows of arrows you type in order before the time bar runs out. Big enemy attacks can be blocked with the same bar. A turn-order strip shows who acts next.
- **Break the enemy.** Good hits fill a stagger meter. When it is full: BREAK. The enemy loses a turn and takes extra damage.
- **Weak points.** Every enemy has something it is weak against and something it resists. The first fights show you which; the Codex remembers.
- **Classes and cards.** Knight, Archer and Mage from the start, and a fourth class waiting for the first player who gets out. Level-ups and chests offer three cards: a new skill, an upgrade or a passive. Take one, or skip it for gold.
- **Stealth.** Enemies on the map only notice you when you are close and in their sight. Slip past them, wait in the tall grass, or pick the fight you want.
- **Beds, a merchant and traps.** Rest or sharpen a skill at a bed (once each), spend your gold at the merchant, open the trapped chest if you dare.
- **Seeds.** Start a run on a random seed, or type your own and play the same dungeon again.
- **Profiles.** Several players can share one PC: every profile has its own three save files, its own Codex and its own unlocks.
- **English and Korean**, keyboard, gamepad and mouse, and a timing assist for wider bar zones.

Short, hard where it should be, and made by two people. Free: pay what you want.

## Features

- Timing-bar and arrow-sequence combat with Perfect / Good / OK / Weak hits
- BREAK stagger, weak points and resistances, status effects with turn counters
- Four classes, a card pick on every level-up and chest
- Stealth on the map: sight lines, tall grass, enemies that give up the chase
- A different run every time: shuffled cards, shops, enemies, chests; optional seed entry
- About 25 to 30 minutes a run, floor after floor of dungeon, a short story and an ending
- Profiles with three save files each, a Codex of enemies, notes and run history
- English and Korean, full keyboard, gamepad and mouse support
- 4-colour 8-bit look, 160x144 world scaled to any window size

## Controls

| | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | W A S D or arrow keys | left stick or d-pad |
| Confirm / talk / interact | Space, Z or Enter | A |
| Back / cancel | Esc or X | B |
| Menu | Tab or I | X or Start |
| Run (hold) | Shift | a trigger |
| Tabs / pages | Q and E | LB and RB |

Mouse: left click confirms, right click goes back, the wheel scrolls lists. Keys can be changed in Settings. In a fight press Confirm when the cursor is in the bright zone; hold Confirm for two seconds to skip a cutscene.

## System requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- A gamepad is optional
- About 100 MB of disk space; no installer (unzip and run "NO HOPE.exe")
- Saves are written to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\DD_Project`; delete the game folder to uninstall
