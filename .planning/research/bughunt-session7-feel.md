## 1. P2: losing focus in the middle of a bar or arrow sequence does NOT freeze it, so alt-tab costs the grade (or the whole hit)
- **Where:** `scripts/scr_menu/scr_menu.gml:3838-3839` (Focus_Step: "a running bar is never interrupted: the loss stays pending until it is over") and `:871-874` (Menu_BattleAllowed refuses while `_qte_busy`). The bar clocks `Battle_PlayerAttackTimingStep` (`scr_combat.gml:1407`), `Battle_SkillQteStep` (`:1491`), `Battle_DefenceStep` (`:2044`) read only `delta_time`; nothing in `objects/obj_battle_controller/Step_0.gml` looks at `window_has_focus()` (the only readers are `obj_ui_controller/Step_0.gml:8` and the display code). The test `fairui_the_bar_clock_keeps_running_over_a_focus_loss` (`scr_debug.gml:7794`) pins the current behaviour.
- **Repro:** fight a Slime, choose Attack, Alt-Tab away at once and come back 3 s later: the cursor has run out, the grade is Weak (0.75 x), the menu opens only afterwards. Worse on a telegraphed enemy attack (Vine Trap, Ramming): Alt-Tab during the "!" and the bar times out with no press, 100 % of the hit lands, on a low-HP run that is a death with no input from the player.
- **Violates:** D122 ("QTE timers and all ms timers frozen ... a QTE resumes exactly where it was"); `PLAYTEST-QUEUE.md` Q-040 step 2 expects "The bar or arrows carry on exactly where they stopped (no lost time)". D140 did not amend D122.
- **Fix idea:** while `!window_has_focus()` (or `global.focus_prev == false`) skip the `qte_ms` / `sq_ms` / `def_ms` advance (an early `return` before `+= Qte_StepMs(...)` in the three Step functions); keep the "menu waits until the bar is over" rule or open the menu over a frozen bar. Add a scenario that holds `Focus_Step(false)` for 600 ms and checks `qte_ms` did not move.

## 4. P3: BREAK by a Perfect hit plays the same sample twice in one frame (the "second layer" is not a layer)
- **Where:** `scripts/scr_combat/scr_combat.gml:1361` (grade sound `qte_perfect` = `Punch_crunchy_2`) and `:733` (Battle_StaggerHit plays `Qte_GradeSfxKey(QTE_PERFECT)` again as "the BREAK sound ... a second layer on top of the grade sound"), both inside the same Step; same for skills (`Battle_SkillGradeFeedback` then StaggerHit) and throws.
- **Repro:** a Perfect hit gives +50 % stagger, so it is the commonest BREAK. Two voices of the identical sample start on the same sample: +6 dB, can clip at SFX 100 %, and sounds like one louder Perfect with no distinct BREAK cue. For a Good or OK hit the layering does work.
- **Violates:** the intent in the comment at :730-732 and D138 (BREAK is a heavier moment with its own feedback).
- **Fix idea:** play the BREAK layer at a lower pitch/volume (e.g. `SFX_Play(key, 0.8, 0.7)`), or skip it when `_perfect` is true and the grade sound already is that sample.
